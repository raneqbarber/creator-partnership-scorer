# Scoring Dimensions

Four dimensions. The first is a gate, not a weight.

```
Fit = (Audience fit × 0.40) + (Delivery × 0.25) + (Trust × 0.25) + (Alignment × 0.10)
```

---

## 1 · Audience-to-ICP fit — the gate

**Score 1 or 2 and the evaluation stops.** A great communicator reaching the wrong people is a great
communicator reaching the wrong people.

| Signal | Strength |
|---|---|
| Audience demographics matching ICP | Strongest, where available |
| Comment content — who is talking, about what | Best free proxy |
| Content topic overlap with the buyer's problem | Moderate |
| Geography and language | A gate, not a score |

**Never infer audience from the creator's own demographics.** A 28-year-old creator may have a
45-year-old audience.

## 2 · Message delivery capability

Can they carry *your* message — different from whether they are good.

- Do they explain, or only react?
- Can they hold a point for the length your message needs?
- Does the format flatten nuance?
- **How do their sponsored posts read against their organic ones?** Most diagnostic. A creator whose
  sponsored content reads visibly differently has an audience that has learned to skip it.

## 3 · Audience trust

Does the audience act on recommendations, or just watch?

Signals: comments asking follow-up questions rather than praising · saves and shares over likes ·
audience referencing past recommendations by name · whether past sponsored posts drew engagement or
silence.

## 4 · Brand alignment — subtracts, never adds

Voice compatibility, values, risk exposure, category conflicts. Scored as a subtractor: misalignment
can disqualify a good-fit creator, but alignment cannot rescue a bad-fit one.

---

## Activation matching

The output is a format, not a yes or no.

| Creator profile | Activation |
|---|---|
| Strong explanation, deep trust | Long-form, tutorial, co-authored |
| Strong reach, moderate trust | Awareness placement |
| Niche audience, exact ICP match | Direct recommendation, gated content |
| **Strong delivery, weak audience fit** | **License their content for your own paid distribution** |

That last row is the most under-used option. Where someone communicates superbly to the wrong
people, licensing buys the craft without paying for the audience.

---

## Confidence

No audience data → infer from comments and content, and **cap at medium confidence**. Say so on
every affected score.
